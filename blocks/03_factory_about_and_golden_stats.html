20 Years of Vietnamese Craftsmanship & Global Reach

Direct Factory Powerhouse in An Giang

Founded in 2006, ATC Furniture has grown over 20 years of relentless dedication into one of Vietnam’s foremost manufacturers and exporters of luxury outdoor wicker, teak, and water hyacinth furniture, operating from our 40,000-square-meter facility in An Giang Province.

Equipped with precision aluminum tube bending, robotic powder-coating, master hand-weaving artisans, and ISO-standard packaging, we export 40+ containers per month to over 50+ countries worldwide.
