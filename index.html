ABOUT ATC FURNITURE (SINCE 2006)

20 Years of Vietnamese Craftsmanship & Global Reach

ATC Furniture is a leading manufacturer and exporter of wicker furniture supplier Vietnam. Founded in 2006 as a humble workshop, we have grown over 20 years of relentless dedication into one of Vietnam’s foremost manufacturers of wicker and teak furniture, operating from our 40,000-square-meter facility in An Giang Province. Today, we proudly export our products to over 50 countries worldwide, bringing the essence of Vietnamese craftsmanship to luxury resorts, 5-star hotels, and premier wholesalers around the globe.

With international design standards, advanced powder-coating lines, artisanal hand-weaving mastery, and comprehensive OEM/ODM engineering, ATC Wicker Furniture provides complete outdoor and indoor living solutions engineered for durability, aesthetics, and tropical resilience.
